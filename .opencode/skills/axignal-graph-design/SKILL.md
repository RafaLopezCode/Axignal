# AXIGNAL Graph Design

Use this skill whenever work builds AXIGLAND graph UI, changes a graph projection
or interaction, introduces/replaces a renderer, changes node or edge grammar,
implements semantic LOD, PATHX visualization, temporal graph UX, corporate
hierarchy, evidence drill-down, representation anomalies, graph accessibility,
or graph performance architecture.

This is AXIGNAL-specific design intelligence, not a generic graph recipe.
AXIGLAND is the canonical, temporal, evidence-backed economic memory. AXIGNAL
is an observing economic brain, not an economic index. A graph is one
demand-materialized cognitive projection, not the final product; explainable
economic intelligence is the product. The purpose is to make meaning legible
before spectacle. A fast hairball is still a hairball; **WOW != volume**.

Follow the authority order: MASTER → Engineering Constitution → accepted ADRs
and contracts → feature spec → design brief → this skill → implementation. ADR-
0009 accepts a hybrid architecture with no foundation framework. AXIGNAL owns
semantic cartography and its renderer contract. Sigma + Graphology is only the
initial replaceable adapter implementation. Neither is AXIGLAND, canonical
truth, or a production dependency by virtue of this skill.

## Design rules

- Keep one canonical world and many query-shaped projections. Perspective is a
  query, not permission or truth mutation.
- Keep observed, potential, historical, inferred, stale, disputed, and unknown
  states distinct. UNKNOWN != FALSE; POTENTIAL != OBSERVED; historical !=
  current. Never rely on color alone.
- Treat relationships as economic objects. Keep nature, direction, materiality,
  epistemic class, currentness, evidence, and decision state separate; never
  collapse them into a generic strength score.
- Surface demand, activity, capability-specific reach and any derived potential
  opportunity through inspectable dimensions and explanation traces. Procurement
  does not imply a customer or relationship; a map alone is not an opportunity
  explanation.
- PATHX is an explainable path through constituent relationships, never an
  implied direct edge between its endpoints.
- Make focus/recenter alter the view, not canonical meaning. Preserve orientation
  and identity through incremental materialization where feasible.
- Make surprising or anomalous representation inspectable, not silently
  suppressed. “Why am I seeing this?” must trace persisted derivation, FAXTs,
  evidence, source lineage, and observation/currentness; do not invent narrative.
- Keep search, generative, social/public-conversation and public
  reputation/experience projections distinct. A review is an observed claim,
  not business truth; a source rating is not an AXIGNAL judgment; an anomaly is
  not fraud. RepresentationGap and ExperienceSignal are derived and must expose
  source, instrument/version, period, sample, coverage and uncertainty.
- Do not naively compare ratings across sources, double-count syndicated
  observations, project product/location experience onto an entire company, or
  let private first-party analytics enter public AXIGLAND projections.
- Leave a REQUEST REVIEW seam. Review does not grant graph editing or canonical
  truth authority; disputed does not mean false. Review states may include
  UPHELD, REVISED, RETIRED, or UNRESOLVED under the separate review policy.
- Demand-materialize only useful context. Semantic LOD considers relevance,
  focus, distance, epistemic and temporal state, corporate hierarchy, PATHX,
  anomalies, evidence, and density; zoom is an input, not the authority.
- Keep labels, accessibility, and product performance budgets AXIGNAL-owned.
  Bakeoff values are measurements from specified harness conditions, not product
  targets or universal guarantees. Unmeasured accessibility is a validation
  requirement, not a proven property.
- Keep Sigma/Graphology types below the renderer adapter. Do not freeze a runtime
  contract schema or implement graph UI under a design-only task.
- Apply `frontend-design` for subordinate visual direction and `ui-ux-pro-max`
  for subordinate usability/accessibility critique. Neither overrides the
  MASTER or accepted architecture. Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, neon AI
  spaghetti, meaningless glow, decorative node clouds, forced 3D, gratuitous
  motion, and generic force-directed hairballs.

## Authority limits

This skill grants no authority to modify canonical economic semantics, evidence
truth/admission, cognition policy, JEV policy, Claim Review policy, or source
acquisition policy. Follow their governing documents and stop if graph design
appears to require changing them. It does not authorize runtime implementation,
deployment, CRM/workflow behavior, or customer edits to graph truth.
