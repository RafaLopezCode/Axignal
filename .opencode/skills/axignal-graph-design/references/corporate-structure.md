# Corporate Structure

Corporate structure has a hierarchy grammar that should not be forced into a
generic force-directed layout. Governed natures can include PARENT_OF,
SUBSIDIARY_OF, BRAND_OF, DIVISION_OF, CONTROLLED_BY, JOINT_VENTURE_WITH, and
AFFILIATED_WITH. Preserve the source relationship's direction and nature; do
not infer ownership or control from visual containment alone.

The map may combine corporate hierarchy with ordinary economic relationships,
but must keep those grammars distinguishable. A parent-child arrangement may
use containment, alignment, or a hierarchy layout while supply and partnership
ties use separate relationship marks. Corporate structure can influence LOD
aggregation and focus context only according to governed semantics and evidence.

The renderer never decides corporate truth. Unknown, disputed, potential, or
time-bounded structure stays explicitly qualified and traceable to evidence.
