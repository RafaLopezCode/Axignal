# Economic Cartography

AXIGLAND is a projection of the observable real economy: temporal, evidence-
backed, demand-materialized, and open to investigation. It models the economic
world; it does not manage the customer's business. A useful map lets an observer
understand identity, context, distance, relationships, paths, evidence, and time
without confusing the map for canonical truth.

Use this semantic travel hierarchy:

```text
WORLD → SECTOR / MACRO ECONOMIC CONTEXT → ECOSYSTEM
      → ECONOMIC NEIGHBOURHOOD → ORGANIZATION
      → RELATIONSHIP / PATHX → EVIDENCE
```

The levels are semantic projections, not required nested database records or
zoom thresholds. Keep location and orientation apparent when moving between
levels. A user can move from broad context to an organization, inspect a
relationship and reach its evidence, then return with the broader context
intact.

FIRST_MAP_WOW means: “You know what your company is. AXIGNAL shows you what the
observable economic world says it is—and why.” Prioritize observable economic
identity, strongest observed relationships, relevant corporate structure,
capabilities and markets, evidence-backed non-obvious connections, clearly
distinguished potential relationships, temporal signals, anomalies, and
explainability. Non-obvious + relevant + evidence-backed + connected +
explainable is valuable. Unsupported surprise is not. Never optimize the first
map for maximum node count.
