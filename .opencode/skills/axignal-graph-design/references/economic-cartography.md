# Economic Cartography

AXIGLAND is governed memory of the observable real economy: temporal, evidence-
backed, demand-materialized, and open to investigation. AXIGNAL is an observing
economic brain, not an economic index. It models the economic world; it does not
manage the customer's business. Economic cartography is cognitive substrate,
not the final product. Economic Opportunity Intelligence and Compounding
Economic Intelligence are the product. A useful projection lets an observer
understand identity, context, demand/activity, capability-specific reach,
relationships, paths, evidence and time without confusing derived opportunity
or the map for canonical truth.

Use this semantic travel hierarchy:

```text
WORLD → SECTOR / MACRO ECONOMIC CONTEXT → ECOSYSTEM
      → ECONOMIC NEIGHBOURHOOD → ORGANIZATION
      → RELATIONSHIP / PATHX → EVIDENCE
```

The levels are semantic projections, not required nested database records or
zoom thresholds. Keep location and orientation apparent when moving between
levels. A user can move from broad context to an organization, inspect a
relationship and reach its evidence, then return with the broader context
intact.

FIRST_MAP_WOW means: “You know what your company is. AXIGNAL shows you what the
observable economic world says it is—and what activity may deserve attention,
why, and with what uncertainty.” Prioritize observable economic identity,
relevant demand/projects, capability-specific reach, evidence-backed observed
relationships, potential opportunities with explicit temporal and epistemic
state, relevant corporate structure, capabilities and markets, non-obvious
connections, temporal signals, anomalies and explanation. A procurement signal
does not imply a customer; capability match does not imply commercial fit.
Non-obvious + relevant + evidence-backed + connected + explainable is valuable.
Unsupported surprise is not. Never optimize the first map for maximum node
count.
