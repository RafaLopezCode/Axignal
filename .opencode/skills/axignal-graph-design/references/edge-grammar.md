# Edge Grammar

Relationships are first-class economic objects. The vocabulary includes
SUPPLIES, DISTRIBUTES, MANUFACTURES_FOR, PARTNERS_WITH, SUBSIDIARY_OF, OWNS,
JOINT_VENTURE_WITH, CUSTOMER_OF, CERTIFIED_BY, and other governed relationship
natures. Show direction when the governed nature is directed; do not invent
direction when it is unknown or not applicable.

Keep independent dimensions independent: relationship nature, epistemic class,
currentness, materiality, direction, evidence, and confidence/decision state.
Never collapse them into one “strength” score. A thicker edge must not silently
mean more certain, more current, or more important. Selected edges can expose
their nature and details without increasing background clutter. Omitted or
attenuated edges remain accessible through expansion/inspection and are never
treated as false.

Edge grammar must distinguish direct relationships from PATHX membership and
corporate hierarchy. Each materially displayed tie needs a route to provenance
and time. If multiple relationships share endpoints, preserve identity and
inspectability rather than merging them into an unexplained line.
