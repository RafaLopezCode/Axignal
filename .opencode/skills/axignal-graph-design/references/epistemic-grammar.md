# Epistemic Grammar

Preserve governed distinctions including OBSERVED, DECLARED, INFERRED,
CORROBORATED, CONTRADICTED, STALE, UNKNOWN and graph-level OBSERVED, POTENTIAL,
HISTORICAL. These categories are not all one mutually exclusive scale; respect
their domain definitions and combinations.

Critical invariants: UNKNOWN != FALSE; DISPUTED != FALSE; POTENTIAL != OBSERVED;
HISTORICAL != CURRENT; INFERRED != OBSERVED; CUSTOMER_DISAGREEMENT !=
CANONICAL_ERROR. No visual encoding may erase one of these distinctions or
promote inference to observation. Color alone is insufficient: pair color with
shape, line pattern, textual label, iconography, or an accessible description.

The challenge seam is REQUEST REVIEW. Review states such as UPHELD, REVISED,
RETIRED, or UNRESOLVED belong to the separately governed review process. They do
not grant customer edit authority, and customer disagreement is not itself proof
of canonical error.

Avoid unsupported numeric precision and do not display internal confidence as
fact. A selected element should state what is known, what is inferred or
potential, and where evidence can be inspected. Representation and styling are
interpretations for observation; neither writes back to evidence admission or
canonical truth.
