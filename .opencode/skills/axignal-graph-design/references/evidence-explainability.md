# Evidence Explainability

Every material or surprising graph element should eventually support this
trace:

```text
Graph element → derivation → FAXTs → evidence → source/source lineage
             → observation time and currentness
```

For an inferred or potential relationship, include its derivation, relevant
structured features, FAXTs, and evidence in that order. This is a persisted provenance
trace, not post-hoc generative storytelling. If a link in the chain is
unavailable or uncertain, say so; do not fabricate the explanation.

Design an inspection seam reachable from the element, with a concise reason for
inclusion and routes to supporting records. Keep observation time distinct from
validity time, and preserve stale/unknown qualifiers. “WHY AM I SEEING THIS?”
does not itself admit evidence, resolve a claim, or modify canonical truth.
Respect evidence admission and source-lineage authorities.
