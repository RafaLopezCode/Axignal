# Focus and Recenter

Focus changes perspective, not canonical truth. Keep explicit context tiers:
CURRENT FOCUS, NEAR CONTEXT, SECONDARY CONTEXT, and BACKGROUND CONTEXT. These are
view roles, not epistemic or domain classes.

A Hop1/Hop2 organization may become a new Hop0 research focus. The transition
should promote it while retaining enough prior context to explain why it was
visible and how it connects to the former focus. Preserve stable positions or
use a legible transition where feasible; avoid destructive layout resets that
discard mental orientation. A recenter must not change edge direction, truth,
evidence, or canonical identity.

Keyboard and assistive interactions need equivalent focus, selection, and
recenter operations with an announced focus description. Provide a deterministic
way back to previous context. Do not assume a click is the only means of
selection or recenter.
