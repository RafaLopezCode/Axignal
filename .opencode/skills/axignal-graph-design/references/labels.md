# Labels

Label policy is an open evidence gap; no candidate's bakeoff result empirically
solved AXIGNAL label legibility. Do not claim a settled algorithm. Define
priorities and test them against real semantic tasks and graph densities.

Candidate priority order: current focus identity; selected nodes and edges;
PATHX constituents; high-relevance near context; corporate hierarchy needed to
understand ownership/brand/division relations; material anomalies; temporal
qualifiers; cluster names; secondary organizations. Evidence labels belong in
inspection surfaces or accessible descriptions, not as a permanent wall of
text. This is a design hypothesis to validate, not an immutable ordering.

Manage collisions and maximum visual density through semantic LOD, selective
labels, stable reveal on focus/selection, and textual alternatives. Do not show
every organization name at every scale. A hidden label must not make an entity
undiscoverable or inaccessible. The selected item’s text must identify entity,
relationship nature where applicable, epistemic/currentness qualifiers, and a
route to evidence.
