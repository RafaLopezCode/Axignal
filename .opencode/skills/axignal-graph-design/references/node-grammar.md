# Node Grammar

An organization node may communicate entity/organization type, relevance,
current focus, graph distance, corporate role, epistemic/currentness state,
temporal activity, cluster membership, anomaly, and observability. Do not encode
every dimension at once. Establish a readable hierarchy: identity and focus
first; entity kind and corporate role next; epistemic/currentness and anomaly
with explicit non-color cues; secondary metadata on selection or inspection.

Use position/containment for meaningful economic or corporate structure only
when the layout semantics are clear. Avoid implying economic importance through
size unless a governed, explainable measure and suitable evidence support it.
Never infer canonical truth from shape, size, color, or cluster membership.

When visual channels conflict, truth and epistemic differentiation take
priority, then selected/focused status and task-relevant relationships, then
decorative refinement. Use labels, shape/pattern, outlines, and textual
descriptions as complementary channels. Keep identity stable when style or LOD
changes. Define exact visual mappings through later validated design, not by
assuming the bakeoff measured semantic legibility.
