# Performance Budgets and Evidence

Keep **MEASURED_BAKEOFF_EVIDENCE** distinct from **PRODUCT_PERFORMANCE_TARGET**.
The bakeoff captured specific harness, fixture, browser, machine, and adapter
conditions. Those numbers are comparative evidence, not user-facing SLAs or
universal guarantees. P0-GRAPH-01R repaired the harness after recording the raw
results: filter and temporal scenario figures are historical pre-repair values,
and the requested +20,000 Sigma expansion had only 3,900 pending nodes on the
final medium-fixture operation. Cosmos was removed from the current harness;
its raw results are historical only. Preserve original JSON/screenshots and use
the bakeoff report's caveats.

The accepted conclusion is provisional headroom for Sigma behind the AXIGNAL
boundary, especially from comparative render/frame evidence. This does not
justify rendering 100k nodes as a product hairball. Semantic projection controls
scope.

Future product budgets must be explicitly set and measured for first meaningful
render, recenter, filter, progressive additions, temporal transitions, PATHX
highlighting, memory, frame time, labels, and large-graph interaction. Record
fixture, hardware/browser, distribution/percentile, warm/cold state, and user
task. Accessibility and label usability have no empirical proof yet and need
their own validation; do not infer them from frame rates.
