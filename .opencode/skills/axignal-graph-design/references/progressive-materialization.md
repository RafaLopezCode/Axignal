# Progressive Materialization

AXIGLAND is demand-materialized as AXENT observes. The graph may gain nodes and
relationships; a relationship may become historical; evidence may become stale
or be reverified; potential state, cluster membership, corporate links,
currentness, and anomalies may change.

Use stable identities and incremental transitions so additions and revisions
do not unnecessarily reset the whole map. Announce material changes with truthful
system state and preserve links from new elements to their evidence and time.
Distinguish newly materialized context from a change in canonical status.
Transition motion must be optional/reduced when requested; the final semantic
state must be understandable without animation.

“Compute once. Learn permanently. Verify when necessary” guides continuity but
does not authorize storing unsupported conclusions. Incremental renderer
capability is not a reason to materialize a hairball; semantic LOD and user
orientation remain AXIGNAL responsibilities.
