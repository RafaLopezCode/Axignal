# Representation Anomalies

An unexpected map result is not automatically an AXIGNAL error. For example, a
semiconductor organization may appear connected to a hair-salon economic
cluster because AXIGNAL reasoning is wrong—or because real public signals
reconstruct its observable context unexpectedly. Do not silently hide a result
solely because it looks odd.

Make anomalous representation inspectable: identify which graph elements or
relationships are surprising, expose epistemic and temporal qualifiers, and
offer the “Why am I seeing this?” provenance path. Keep anomaly status separate
from canonical error, customer disagreement, and falsehood. A challenge is a
request to investigate, not an edit to the graph. Anomaly detection, authority,
and resolution policy remain governed elsewhere; the renderer must not assign
canonical anomaly truth.
