# Semantic Level of Detail

Semantic LOD is AXIGNAL's choice of what economic meaning a projection should
show, summarize, or make drillable. It is not merely hiding nodes at low zoom.
Zoom can inform the decision, but cannot decide it alone.

Consider focus, graph distance, economic relevance/materiality, epistemic and
temporal state, corporate hierarchy, PATHX participation, anomaly importance,
evidence importance, and visual/information density. Preserve stable identity
through aggregate, expand, and collapse transitions. Aggregates should disclose
what they summarize and offer a route to constituents; collapsing must not
discard canonical facts, evidence, or path membership.

Use separate, empirically validated budgets for visible nodes, edges, labels,
and clusters. Prefer retaining focused entities, meaningful observed ties,
selected PATHX constituents, material anomalies, and evidence access over
uniform sampling. If a budget forces omission, indicate that more context exists
and provide deterministic expansion. Maintain focus and PATHX continuity across
temporal transitions. Do not let renderer capacity set the product's semantic
scope or silently turn omitted/unknown relationships into negatives.

No runtime algorithm, threshold, or exact projection schema is selected here.
