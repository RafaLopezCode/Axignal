# Temporal Grammar

Time is intrinsic to AXIGLAND. Respect `valid_from`, `valid_until`,
`first_observed_at`, `last_observed_at`, `last_verified_at`, currentness, and
temporal transition semantics defined by the domain. Historical ties can remain
visible in a historical projection without implying that they are current.

Render current, historical, stale, and unknown-currentness states distinctly
and textually. Do not invent an unknown end date or imply that observation time
equals validity time. Show the relevant time basis when inspecting a tie.
Temporal comparison should preserve mental orientation where possible and make
added, ended, changed, stale, or unverifiable elements inspectable.

The long-term design should support reconstruction at different times while
representing limits in the evidence. A smooth animation is not evidence of
continuous knowledge; reduced-motion and non-animated comparison alternatives
must remain possible. Temporal transitions do not authorize rewriting canonical
history.
