# ADR-0016: Human First Cognitive UX Is a Core AXIGNAL Product Capability

- **Status:** Accepted product/interaction doctrine; pre-implementation
- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Authority:** MASTER §55; Engineering Constitution XIX and XXII
- **Scope:** subscriber-facing human output, semantic navigation and evidence access

## Context

AXIGNAL can govern more evidence, relationships, derived intelligence and
temporal changes than a person can manually integrate. A correct economic
brain still fails as a product if a subscriber must learn internal ontology,
remember where information lives, compare disconnected values from memory, or
trust fluent but unverifiable explanation. Existing Subscriber Experience
modes remain Today, Explore, Evolution, Evidence and Ask AXENT.

External HCI research offers bounded constraints, not proof of an AXIGNAL
outcome. See
[`HFX_COGNITIVE_PSYCHOLOGY_AND_HCI_RESEARCH.md`](../research/HFX_COGNITIVE_PSYCHOLOGY_AND_HCI_RESEARCH.md)
and the user-validation protocol. “Cognitive Jevons,” Interpretation Debt and
Human Cognitive Amortization are AXIGNAL product hypotheses/concepts, not
established empirical facts.

## Decision

Treat Human First cognitive UX as a core product capability that transfers
governed economic intelligence into human understanding while preserving
canonical meaning and inspectability. Canonical requirements:

- Cognitive Compression preserves material epistemic, temporal, scope,
  denominator, contradiction and evidence context.
- Semantic depth is `GLANCE → UNDERSTAND → REASON → PROVE`, directly navigable,
  not a required wizard; the same object and truth serve all expertise levels.
- Navigate by human meaning/relationship without requiring ontology or menu
  memory.
- Present meaning before metrics; explain measures and their interpretation
  boundaries where material.
- Perform joins AXIGNAL already governs, retaining dependencies and provenance.
- Every material output has a path to real derivation/evidence; explanation is
  not post-hoc generative storytelling.
- Preserve independent semantic-family, epistemic, temporal, attention and
  output-archetype dimensions. `UNKNOWN != FALSE`, `POTENTIAL != OBSERVED`, and
  `HISTORICAL != CURRENT`.
- Same canonical truth across explanation densities; attention does not create
  economic truth or relevance.
- Human comprehension/accessibility is evaluated with representative users;
  preference, visual polish and compliance alone do not prove it.

## Alternatives considered

- **Cosmetic design-only treatment:** rejected; leaves navigation, interpretation
  and evidence burden unmanaged.
- **Expert and beginner products with separate truth:** rejected; fragments
  meaning and risks changing epistemic presentation.
- **Metrics-first dashboard:** rejected; does not itself explain meaning,
  scope, uncertainty or joins.
- **Chat as the primary product:** rejected; ordinary comprehension cannot
  require a prompt, and AXENT explanation must remain evidence-grounded.
- **Freeze visual grammar now:** rejected; color/icon/layout encodings remain
  hypotheses until validated.

## Consequences

- MASTER §55 and this ADR govern human-facing meaning; the HFX doctrine is a
  subordinate operational explanation.
- Subscriber Experience is converged in place; no replacement navigation spec
  is created.
- Future prototypes must test meaning, epistemic understanding, expert depth,
  evidence access, resumption, trust calibration and cognitive accessibility.
- No UI, schema, runtime, provider, analytics or persistence implementation is
  authorized by this ADR.

## Tradeoffs

Meaning-first projections require design and validation across user expertise
levels and can expose that AXIGNAL lacks sufficient evidence. Preserving the
truthful unknown is preferable to using simple but misleading certainty. Direct
depth access increases interaction-path requirements but avoids forcing a
single linear depth sequence.

## Non-decision

This ADR selects no layout, color, icon, chart, graph renderer, density
adaptation, information threshold or success target. It does not validate the
product hypotheses or authorize a production surface.
