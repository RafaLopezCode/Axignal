# ADR-0093 — Governed fixed-currency commercial pricing (EUR/USD/GBP)

**Status:** Accepted for defensive price-book foundation; foreign-currency activation blocked pending commercial approval and verified Stripe integration.
**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Authority:** MASTER §27, Constitution, ADR-0061, ADR-0065, ADR-0067, ADR-0068, ADR-0084.

## Context

AXIGNAL markets internationally. MASTER §27 approves EUR 9.95/month for the first observed Organization and EUR 4.95/month for each additional Organization. Browser language and geography do not authorize USD or GBP amounts. A currency symbol change without changing the payment contract is false advertising.

## Decision

1. Introduce a typed versioned fixed-price book. EUR 995/495 minor units are the only published prices. Foreign offers require explicit positive amounts, an approval reference and unique complete Stripe Price references for both base and additional organizations.
2. An authorized billing country may suggest USD (US) or GBP (GB), but the choice falls back to EUR when the exact foreign offer is unavailable. Locale formats a number; it does not change denomination.
3. Never obtain contractual pricing from dynamic FX, browser IP, text generated by an AI or unverified client input. All provider Price metadata, recurring terms, currency, tax policy and amounts require server validation before a charge.
4. The billing currency for an existing subscription must be preserved across expansions, prorations, reconciliation, refunds and historical invoices. Multi-currency monetary amounts cannot be added without a separate approved conversion authority.
5. Foreign-currency activation requires a separate approved commercial price decision, validated Stripe live Price objects, currency-bound tenant purchase intents, persistent subscription currency, provider payment verification, legal/tax approval, test/CI gates and real authorized production E2E.
6. **This change DOES NOT enable USD/GBP charges.** Current checkout remains strictly EUR and contracting remains disabled. The defensive catalog, unit tests and shared euro presentation remove duplication without weakening billing authority.

## Acceptance

- EUR remains 995/495 in UI, Checkout and Admin.
- Unapproved/partial/duplicate/unknown foreign currency offers fail closed.
- Browser language cannot trigger a different currency or payment.
- Research costs, billing receipts and financial ledgers preserve source currency.
- Passing price-book tests does not imply foreign Checkout, Stripe subscription, tax or accounting E2E.
