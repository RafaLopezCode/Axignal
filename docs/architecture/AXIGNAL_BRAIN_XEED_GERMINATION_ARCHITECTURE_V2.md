**Scope:** Cognitive compute architecture for planting, germinating, evolving and continuously cultivating a Xeed into AXIGLAND.

## 1. Executive thesis

AXIGNAL must not be implemented as a headless collection of scrapers, prompts and graph components. Its defensible core is a persistent **economic intelligence brain** that decides what must be learned, acquires evidence, converts observations into structured state, evaluates uncertainty, spends compute where information gain is highest, materializes reusable economic knowledge and continuously maintains AXIGLAND.

The unit of customer intent is the **Xeed**.

> **Planting a Xeed authorizes AXIGNAL to dedicate autonomous compute to independently observe, understand and cultivate an organization's economic environment.**

A Xeed does not create a company profile, grant authority over a canonical node or inject claims into AXIGLAND. It creates a persistent observation objective.

The brain must optimize for:

> **maximum reusable, verifiable economic information gain per unit of money, time and compute.**

The primary cognitive execution policy is **GPT-6 Luna through the OpenAI Batch endpoint**, with AXIGNAL designed explicitly around asynchronous batch semantics rather than treating Batch as a cheaper drop-in transport.

The economic advantage of Batch is to be reinvested in **depth of intelligence**: broader discovery, corroboration, contradiction search, temporal verification, selective multi-hop exploration and targeted uncertainty-reduction loops.

The Brain is not authorized to invent an internal model of the company from public traces. Its germination target is the **externally observable economic representation** of the organization: how it appears, connects, changes and can be reconstructed from independent public evidence.

> **You know what your company is. AXIGNAL shows you what the observable economic world says it is — and why.**

The questions used during germination are therefore an **internal research program**, not a customer-facing questionnaire and not a collection of deterministic answers. Their outputs are materialized as FAXTs, relationships, INXIGHTs, PATHXs, representation signals, anomalies, evidence lineages and explicit unknowns inside AXIGLAND.

## 2. Core principles

1. **One canonical AXIGLAND.** There is one shared economic world; users observe it from different perspectives.
2. **Xeed buys observation, not influence.** Users direct attention, never conclusions.
3. **Independent evidence only.** User context can define a research objective but cannot become canonical truth without independent evidence.
4. **Demand-materialized intelligence.** AXIGNAL has a global ontology but spends deep compute where demand creates value.
5. **Compute once, learn permanently, verify when necessary.** Reusable knowledge must reduce future marginal compute.
6. **Batch-first cognition.** Cognitive jobs are planned for asynchronous GPT-6 Luna Batch execution unless a separately authorized architecture decision creates an exception.
7. **Depth before latency.** FIRST_MAP_WOW optimizes information quality under an acceptable asynchronous window, not chatbot response speed.
8. **Unknown is not false.** Missing evidence remains epistemically explicit.
9. **Observed is not potential.** Evidence-backed reality and inferred compatibility are distinct graph layers.
10. **Relationship is not PATHX.** A PATHX is an explainable economic path through relationships, not another relationship type.
11. **Time belongs to economic state.** Currentness, first/last observation and validity are first-class.
12. **Models interpret; Python computes; JEV evaluates structured decisions; AXIGNAL owns policy.**
13. **Providers are replaceable organs.** Model, source acquisition and graph renderer must sit behind AXIGNAL-owned boundaries.
14. **LIVE is not DONE.** Passing map readiness begins continuous cultivation rather than ending analysis.
15. **Every background compute unit should maximize useful knowledge gain.**
16. **External observability boundary.** AXIGNAL must not pretend to know private economics, CRM state, internal strategy, margins, departmental performance or other non-observable facts.
17. **Questions drive research; AXIGLAND carries the answers.** Germination questions are internal cognitive scaffolding, not the primary product surface.
18. **Every material claim is explainable.** A subscriber must be able to ask `WHY AM I SEEING THIS?` and traverse the real derivation to evidence.
19. **Every claim is challengeable; no claim is customer-editable.** Review requests trigger independent reinvestigation, never direct canonical mutation.
20. **Evidence, not agreement.** AXIGNAL optimizes for evidence rather than confirming either its prior conclusion or the subscriber's expectation.
21. **Unexpected results are investigation points.** An apparently absurd result may reveal either a Brain error or a real public-representation defect; AXIGNAL must distinguish them through evidence.
22. **No hidden internal-business inference.** Where the required truth depends on CRM, ERP, accounts, contracts, emails or private operational data, canonical public-economic state remains `UNKNOWN_PRIVATE` unless a future separately governed private-context product explicitly provides it.

Never double token usage merely because tokens are cheaper. Spend only when expected marginal information gain justifies the cost.

## 6. GPT-6 Luna Batch architecture

### 6.1 Batch is an architectural primitive

AXIGNAL must not call the model as if every cognitive question were an interactive chat turn. The Brain creates **CognitiveJobs**, batches compatible work, persists correlation metadata and asynchronously ingests results.

```text
CognitiveJob {
    job_id
    xeed_id
    organization_id
    job_type
    objective
    structured_context_ref
    evidence_refs[]
    expected_schema
    priority
    estimated_information_gain
    estimated_compute_cost
    reuse_potential
    freshness_need
    dependencies[]
    status
}
```

### 6.2 Batch Planner

Responsibilities:

- deduplicate semantically equivalent jobs;
- avoid re-analysis of already canonical reusable evidence;
- identify stale knowledge requiring verification;
- group compatible jobs;
- create deterministic correlation IDs / `custom_id` mapping;
- persist every request before submission;
- maintain input/result provenance;
- enforce model/context/output budgets;
- enforce source/evidence boundaries;
- split oversized workloads safely;
- reconcile out-of-order asynchronous results by identity, never position;
- retry only according to explicit failure semantics;
- distinguish transport failure from epistemic uncertainty;
- prevent duplicate canonical writes.

### 6.3 Batch states

AXIGNAL should map provider-specific states into an internal provider-neutral contract. Provider state must not leak into canonical domain semantics.

```text
PLANNED
PREPARED
SUBMITTED
QUEUED
RUNNING
RESULT_AVAILABLE
INGESTED
FAILED_RETRYABLE
FAILED_TERMINAL
CANCELLED
EXPIRED
```

### 6.4 Idempotency

A repeated Batch result must never create duplicate FAXTs, relationships or temporal events.

Canonical writes require stable identities/fingerprints derived from the relevant observation/evidence/claim semantics rather than batch order.

## 7. Economic Intelligence Question Graph & observability boundary

Before the Knowledge Frontier can decide what is missing, AXIGNAL needs a governed model of **what it is legitimate and valuable to investigate**.

The Brain must not use a fixed questionnaire whose textual answers become a report. It maintains an **Economic Intelligence Question Graph (EIQG)**: a recursive graph of research objectives whose answers materialize as economic state.

```text
XEED
  ↓
ECONOMIC INTELLIGENCE MODEL
  ↓
INTELLIGENCE QUESTION GRAPH
  ├── observable
  ├── derivable
  ├── unresolved
  └── private / non-observable
          ↓
KNOWLEDGE FRONTIER
          ↓
RESEARCH PLANNER
          ↓
EVIDENCE + COMPUTE
          ↓
FAXTs / RELATIONSHIPS / REPRESENTATION SIGNALS
          ↓
INXIGHT / PATHX / TEMPORAL STATE
          ↓
AXIGLAND
```

### 7.1 Three epistemic territories

#### A. OBSERVABLE

AXIGNAL may independently investigate public-economic phenomena such as:

- organization identity and corporate structure;
- public economic identity;
- products and services;
- capabilities;
- certifications;
- technologies where publicly evidenced;
- geographies;
- markets publicly addressed;
- observed customers, suppliers, distributors and partners;
- integrations and ecosystem memberships;
- competitors and substitutes supported by observable overlap;
- observed demand and supply signals;
- temporal changes and historical relationships;
- economic neighbourhoods;
- public self-representation;
- external representation by third parties;
- evidence lineage and currentness.

#### B. DERIVABLE FROM OBSERVABLE STATE

AXIGNAL may compute or infer explicitly typed interpretations such as:

- economic position;
- relational proximity;
- capability ↔ demand compatibility;
- market adjacency;
- competitive overlap;
- supply-chain position;
- ecosystem concentration;
- relationship diversity;
- observability;
- representation coverage/gaps;
- temporal momentum;
- structural dependencies visible in the graph;
- PATHXs;
- non-obvious connections;
- recurring observable economic flywheels.

Derived state must preserve its derivation and must never be presented as directly observed fact.

#### C. PRIVATE / NON-OBSERVABLE

AXIGNAL must not pretend to know from public evidence alone:

- profit or margin;
- customer profitability;
- real sales pipeline;
- cash flow;
- internal cost structure;
- contract terms not publicly available;
- internal churn;
- departmental performance;
- sales conversion;
- private strategy;
- internal email sentiment/content;
- CRM/ERP state;
- private operational priorities.

The canonical public-economic result is:

`UNKNOWN_PRIVATE`

not an LLM guess.

### 7.2 The internal germination questions

The Brain should investigate questions such as:

1. **What organization is this, and which public entities belong to the same economic/corporate identity?**
2. **What economic identity does the organization publicly project?**
3. **What products, services and capabilities can be independently observed?**
4. **Which markets, geographies and buyer categories are publicly addressed?**
5. **Which economic relationships are independently observable, with what evidence and currentness?**
6. **Where does the organization sit in observable supply, distribution, partnership and corporate structures?**
7. **Which organizations compete for materially similar needs, buyers, capabilities or positions?**
8. **Which capability-demand adjacencies exist without evidence of a direct relationship?**
9. **Which non-obvious one-hop or multi-hop PATHXs make the organization economically connected to other clusters?**
10. **Which relationships or representations have changed over time?**
11. **Which important claims are weak, stale, contradictory or derived from a single evidence lineage?**
12. **What does the organization say about itself, what does the external ecosystem say about it, and where do those representations diverge?**
13. **Which concepts or clusters appear anomalous relative to the rest of its observable economic footprint?**
14. **What remains genuinely unknown, unobservable or privately unknowable?**
15. **What newly discovered answer opens the next highest-value research question?**

These questions are not a checklist that must always produce an answer. They are research objectives with epistemic outcomes.

### 7.3 Recursive question expansion

The EIQG is a graph, not a list.

```text
discover capability
      ↓
opens market questions
      ↓
opens demand-cluster questions
      ↓
opens organization questions
      ↓
opens relationship questions
      ↓
opens currentness / contradiction questions
```

A discovered subsidiary can open a corporate-structure branch. A certification can open compatible-market branches. A product can open competitor and buyer branches. A surprising relationship can open an evidence-lineage branch.

The Research Planner should expand only where expected information value justifies additional acquisition/compute.

### 7.4 Intelligence coverage

The Brain should maintain coverage by dimension without confusing coverage with truth:

```text
RESOLVED
PARTIALLY_RESOLVED
CONTRADICTED
STALE
UNKNOWN
UNKNOWN_PRIVATE
NOT_OBSERVABLE
```

A readiness decision may accept some explicit unknowns. AXIGNAL must prefer an honest unknown over a fabricated complete map.

### 7.5 Non-obvious information gain

FIRST_MAP_WOW should reward defensible non-obvious connections rather than raw volume.

Conceptually:

```text
WOW VALUE ∝
    EconomicRelevance
  × EvidenceStrength
  × ConnectionValue
  × NonObviousness
  × Explainability
```

This is not a frozen scoring formula. It is an optimization doctrine.

A surprising claim without evidence is low value. An obvious claim with excellent evidence may be necessary but not sufficient for WOW. A non-obvious, economically relevant, explainable connection backed by evidence is a high-value germination fruit.

## 8. Observable representation, anomalies and economic flywheels

AXIGNAL's most valuable result may sometimes look wrong at first glance.

If a semiconductor company appears connected to a hair-salon cluster, AXIGNAL must not silently hide the result and must not insist that the subscriber is wrong. It must expose **why the result exists**.

### 8.1 Representation model

AXIGNAL distinguishes:

```text
PUBLIC SELF-REPRESENTATION
        +
EXTERNAL PUBLIC EVIDENCE
        +
OBSERVED ECONOMIC RELATIONSHIPS
        ↓
AXIGNAL OBSERVABLE ECONOMIC REPRESENTATION
```

The subscriber may know the organization's intended identity. AXIGNAL does not need to know that intention to reveal a mismatch.

This creates a powerful comparison:

```text
INTENDED IDENTITY        ← known by organization/agency
        ↕
OBSERVABLE REPRESENTATION ← independently reconstructed by AXIGNAL
```

AXIGNAL reports the observable side and its evidence. The human decides whether the difference is desirable, accidental or strategically important.

### 8.2 RepresentationSignal

Conceptual object:

```text
RepresentationSignal {
    organization_id
    concept
    source_evidence[]
    external_corroboration[]
    derivation
    epistemic_class
    first_observed_at
    last_observed_at
    currentness
}
```

### 8.3 RepresentationAnomaly

A `RepresentationAnomaly` means that a concept, cluster, relationship or path is structurally unexpected relative to the rest of the observable representation.

It does **not** mean "AXIGNAL has proven the company is wrong."

Possible causes include:

- an AXIGNAL reasoning/classification error;
- entity collision;
- stale evidence;
- ambiguous public wording;
- misleading structured metadata;
- legacy/discontinued product;
- distributor/category contamination;
- third-party misclassification;
- genuine but surprising economic adjacency;
- a real public communication/representation defect.

Anomaly detection may be deterministic/statistical where appropriate. Interpretation remains evidence-governed.

### 8.4 `WHY AM I SEEING THIS?`

Every material graph element should support an evidence trace:

```text
GRAPH ELEMENT
      ↓
DERIVATION / DECISION
      ↓
FAXTs / STRUCTURED FEATURES
      ↓
EVIDENCE
      ↓
SOURCE + LINEAGE
      ↓
OBSERVATION / VERIFICATION TIME
```

For inferred/potential state, the UI must explicitly show that the path is derived rather than observed.

The explanation must be reconstructed from persisted provenance. It must never be a post-hoc LLM story.

### 8.5 Observable EconomicFlywheel

AXIGNAL may detect recurring **observable economic circulation structures** around an organization, but must not prescribe an internal money-making strategy it cannot know.

Conceptual object:

```text
EconomicFlywheel {
    focal_organization
    observable_capabilities[]
    observable_outputs[]
    demand_clusters[]
    enabling_relationships[]
    downstream_relationships[]
    reinforcing_paths[]
    observable_constraints[]
    evidence[]
    currentness
    temporal_evolution
}
```

This answers:

> **How does observable economic value appear to circulate around this organization?**

It does not answer:

> **How should this company run its internal business to maximize profit?**

Unknown or potential segments remain explicitly typed:

```text
OBSERVED → OBSERVED → UNKNOWN → POTENTIAL → OBSERVED
```

No missing segment may be hallucinated merely to complete a visually attractive flywheel.

## 9. Claim Review — challenge without canonical edit authority

Every material AXIGNAL claim is challengeable. No subscriber can directly edit canonical AXIGLAND.

Doctrine:

> **Every claim is challengeable. No claim is customer-editable.**

> **A review request triggers investigation, not modification.**

> **AXIGNAL optimizes for evidence, not agreement.**

Customer-facing principle:

> **AXIGNAL is built to help you see your business more clearly — not to tell you what you want to hear.**

Review explanation:

> **You can challenge anything AXIGNAL observes. We'll investigate it again. Our goal isn't to defend our previous conclusion or confirm yours — it's to follow the evidence.**

### 9.1 Review workflow

```text
SUBSCRIBER DISPUTES CLAIM
        ↓
CLAIM_REVIEW_REQUEST
        ├── reason
        ├── optional explanation
        └── optional submitted evidence / URL
        ↓
EPISTEMIC FIREWALL
        ↓
AXENT ADVERSARIAL REINVESTIGATION
        ├── original evidence
        ├── original derivation
        ├── fresh independent evidence
        ├── contradictory evidence
        ├── currentness checks
        └── submitted material as non-authoritative evidence
        ↓
STRUCTURED REVIEW STATE
        ↓
JEV + DETERMINISTIC POLICY
        ↓
UPHELD | REVISED | RETIRED | UNRESOLVED
```

Definitions:

- **UPHELD** — current evidence continues to support the canonical claim.
- **REVISED** — independent reinvestigation supports a materially different canonical representation.
- **RETIRED** — the claim was previously supported but is no longer sufficiently current/relevant.
- **UNRESOLVED** — available evidence cannot support a sufficiently governed conclusion.

Critical semantics:

`DISPUTED != FALSE`

`UNKNOWN != FALSE`

`CUSTOMER_SAYS_WRONG != CANONICAL_WRONG`

### 9.2 Review data model

Conceptually:

```text
ClaimReview {
    review_id
    claim_id
    requested_by
    requested_at
    reason
    subscriber_statement
    submitted_evidence_refs[]
    original_claim_snapshot
    original_evidence_refs[]
    reinvestigation_jobs[]
    fresh_evidence_refs[]
    contradictory_evidence_refs[]
    decision
    decision_policy_version
    decided_at
}
```

A subscriber statement is context for investigation, never canonical evidence by itself.

### 9.3 Append-oriented history

Claims should not be silently overwritten.

```text
CLAIM v1
  ↓
REVIEW REQUESTED
  ↓
REINVESTIGATION
  ↓
REVIEW DECISION
  ↓
CLAIM v2 / RETIRED / UNCHANGED
```

The evidence ledger should preserve what AXIGNAL observed, why it concluded what it did, what was challenged, what new evidence appeared and why canonical state changed or remained.

### 9.4 Review quality flywheel

Review requests create valuable **attention signals** exactly where humans perceive a possible weakness:

```text
surprising claim
      ↓
human challenge
      ↓
adversarial reinvestigation
      ↓
evidence-backed review outcome
      ↓
calibration dataset
      ↓
better source / decision / anomaly policies
      ↓
better future germination
```

The learnable signal is **not** the subscriber's opinion.

It is the tuple:

```text
DISPUTE
+ INDEPENDENT REINVESTIGATION
+ EVIDENCE OUTCOME
+ GOVERNED DECISION
```

This prevents popularity, customer pressure or coordinated manipulation from becoming truth authority.

## 10. Knowledge Frontier

The **Knowledge Frontier** is one of the central pieces of AXIGNAL IP.

It represents the boundary between what AXIGNAL currently knows with sufficient evidence and what would be economically useful to learn next.

It should be queryable at organization, relationship, market, capability and Xeed scope.

Conceptually:

```text
KnowledgeGap {
    subject
    question
    decision_class
    current_evidence
    missing_information
    uncertainty
    economic_relevance
    expected_information_gain
    expected_cost
    reuse_potential
    freshness_need
    candidate_sources
    priority
}
```

### 10.1 Research priority

A starting scheduling heuristic:

```text
Priority(job) =
    InformationGain
    × EconomicRelevance
    × ReusePotential
    × FreshnessNeed
    / ExpectedComputeCost
```

This is a product hypothesis, not a permanently frozen formula. It must eventually be calibrated against real outcomes.

### 10.2 Stop conditions

AXENT must know when **not** to research further.

Stop or defer when:

- marginal information gain is below threshold;
- sufficient evidence already exists;
- additional sources are redundant;
- source cost exceeds expected economic value;
- source rights/status are not permitted;
- graph expansion exceeds Xeed budget;
- evidence cannot reasonably resolve the uncertainty;
- another active Xeed/job is already acquiring equivalent reusable knowledge.

but it must be interpreted alongside evidence quality and rights, not optimized blindly.

## 13. Python deterministic intelligence

Python is not a substitute for semantic reasoning. It is the deterministic computational substrate around semantic reasoning.

### Python owns

- normalization;
- canonicalization;
- schema validation;
- units/currency/date conversion;
- domain/identifier normalization;
- deterministic deduplication;
- entity-resolution features;
- evidence fingerprints;
- temporal interval operations;
- graph traversal;
- graph algorithms;
- PATHX construction algorithms;
- filtering;
- clustering inputs/features where deterministic;
- feature engineering;
- batching;
- caching;
- queue orchestration;
- deterministic ranking components;
- state construction for JEV;
- invariant enforcement;
- idempotency;
- provenance linkage.

### Python must not

- invent semantic truth;
- convert missing evidence into false;
- silently infer relationship nature without an authorized decision process;
- erase contradictory evidence;
- collapse observed and potential relationships;
- fabricate temporal endpoints.

Doctrine:

## 14. JEV decision layer

JEV receives structured state rather than raw internet chaos whenever possible.

Its role is to evaluate bounded decisions whose inputs have already been normalized and whose possible outputs are typed.

Candidate decision classes:

- entity identity/resolution;
- relationship nature;
- observed vs potential classification;
- currentness assessment;
- corporate-link classification;
- evidence sufficiency;
- contradiction resolution policy inputs;
- map-readiness subdecisions.

### 14.1 No universal 80% threshold

`0.80` can be a prototype heuristic, not doctrine.

Thresholds must eventually be calibrated per decision class against held-out/evaluated data:

```text
threshold(entity_resolution)      = calibrated(A)
threshold(relationship_nature)    = calibrated(B)
threshold(observed_vs_potential)  = calibrated(C)
threshold(currentness)            = calibrated(D)
threshold(corporate_structure)    = calibrated(E)
```

### 14.2 Uncertainty loop

```text
STRUCTURED STATE
      ↓
     JEV
      │
 ┌────┴─────────────┐
 │                  │
sufficient       insufficient
 │                  │
 ▼                  ▼
policy check    missing-information model
 │                  │
 ▼                  ▼
canonical       Knowledge Frontier
candidate            │
                     ▼
               targeted research
                     │
                     └──→ JEV again
```

Low determinism is not automatically equivalent to missing data; it may also indicate ambiguity, contradictory evidence, poor decision framing or intrinsically uncertain reality. The loop must preserve these distinctions.

## 24. Failure semantics

The Brain must distinguish operational failure from epistemic uncertainty.

Examples:

|---|---|---|
| Source unavailable | acquisition failure | retry/fallback/defer |
| Batch transport failure | compute failure | retry according to policy |
| Batch expired | execution not completed | reschedule safely |
| Evidence absent | unknown | preserve UNKNOWN |
| Evidence contradictory | epistemic conflict | contradiction research / retain conflict |
| JEV insufficient | decision uncertainty | diagnose missing/ambiguous inputs |
| Entity collision | resolution uncertainty | targeted disambiguation |
| Map readiness fail | insufficient first-map quality | targeted repair jobs |

Never translate infrastructure failure into semantic falsehood.

## 27. Security, rights and trust boundaries

The Brain must assume acquired material may be adversarial, misleading or malformed.

Requirements:

- preserve source provenance;
- isolate untrusted content from system instructions;
- treat web text as evidence, never instructions;
- validate all structured model output;
- prevent prompt injection from becoming tool authority;
- source capability rights fail closed;
- secrets never enter evidence/model context unless explicitly required and authorized;
- authenticated source sessions must be isolated and governed;
- canonical writes require authorized pipeline stages;
- all material decisions should be auditable back to evidence and policy.

Hostile attention can cause AXIGNAL to observe more; it must not allow an attacker to rewrite canonical truth.

## 32. Germination strategy

A first Xeed should not indiscriminately crawl the internet. Suggested phases:

### Phase G0 — Resolve
Identify the correct organization and eliminate obvious entity ambiguity.

### Phase G1 — Establish Observable Economic DNA
Discover enough evidence to reconstruct what the organization publicly appears to be and do without inventing private internal economics.

### Phase G2 — Establish Representation & Context
Capabilities, products/services, markets, geography, certifications, corporate structure, public self-representation, external representation and evidence lineage.

### Phase G3 — Establish Observed Network
Find evidence-backed economic/corporate relationships and their temporal/currentness state.

### Phase G4 — Expand the Intelligence Question Graph
Use discoveries to open only the highest-value recursive research questions.

### Phase G5 — Potential / PATHX / Observable Flywheel Layer
Evaluate compatible relationships, non-obvious paths and recurring observable economic circulation structures separately from observed reality.

### Phase G6 — Anomaly, Contradiction & Currentness
Detect surprising representation signals, try to falsify important conclusions, collapse duplicate evidence lineages and verify currentness.

### Phase G7 — Explainability & Private-Boundary Audit
Ensure material results can answer `WHY AM I SEEING THIS?` and that no private/internal-business claim has been guessed from insufficient public evidence.

### Phase G8 — Readiness Repair
Translate readiness failures into targeted jobs rather than restarting analysis.

### Phase G9 — Fruit
Publish the first LIVE map only when the representation is sufficiently rich, explainable and epistemically bounded; notify the subscriber.

### Phase G10 — Cultivation & Review
Adaptive monitoring, temporal updates, demand-driven expansion and subscriber-triggered Claim Review.

## 38. Architecture invariants

The following should eventually become enforceable contracts/tests where technically possible:

1. No user-provided claim can directly become canonical FAXT.
2. Every canonical material claim retains evidence provenance.
3. Unknown never silently becomes false.
4. Potential never silently becomes observed.
5. Historical never silently becomes current.
6. PATHX never silently becomes a direct relationship.
7. Provider-specific IDs/states do not define canonical domain identity.
8. Batch results are reconciled by stable identity, never result ordering.
9. Duplicate/retried cognitive results are idempotent.
10. Infrastructure failure never mutates semantic truth.
11. Private subscriber context never silently mutates canonical AXIGLAND.
12. Model output is validated before deterministic/canonical use.
13. Source content never has instruction authority over AXENT.
14. Research jobs have explicit budgets and stopping conditions.
15. Reusable canonical knowledge is preferred over recomputation when still valid.
16. Map readiness failure generates targeted gaps rather than cosmetic success.
17. `LIVE` never means permanently complete.
18. Notification failure is isolated from map readiness.
19. Canonical truth quality is not reduced by subscription tier.
20. AXIGNAL owns the Knowledge Frontier, evidence policy, temporal semantics and canonical graph.
21. Public evidence cannot be used to fabricate private/internal-business truth.
22. `UNKNOWN_PRIVATE` cannot silently become an inferred fact.
23. Germination questions are internal research objectives; their textual answers are not canonical product truth.
24. Every material graph claim must be traceable through derivation to evidence and source lineage.
25. `WHY AM I SEEING THIS?` must use persisted provenance, not post-hoc generated rationale.
26. Representation anomaly does not mean canonical error.
27. Subscriber disagreement cannot directly mutate a FAXT, Relationship, RepresentationSignal or canonical claim.
28. Claim Review must trigger independent reinvestigation through the epistemic firewall.
29. `DISPUTED != FALSE`.
30. Review learning may use evidence-backed review outcomes, never subscriber opinion as ground truth.
31. Observable EconomicFlywheel may represent graph circulation but cannot prescribe private profit strategy without authorized private evidence.
32. The Brain must preserve the distinction between self-representation, external representation and AXIGNAL-derived representation.

## 40. Definition of a successful Brain

The architecture succeeds when planting a previously unseen Xeed causes AXIGNAL to autonomously:

1. resolve the correct organization;
2. determine what it needs to learn;
3. choose permitted, economical sources;
4. acquire independently observed evidence;
5. preserve provenance;
6. structure and normalize evidence deterministically;
7. allocate GPT-6 Luna Batch compute intelligently;
8. evaluate bounded uncertainty with JEV;
9. open targeted research loops when information is insufficient;
10. stop spending when marginal knowledge value falls below cost/budget;
11. materialize reusable canonical knowledge into AXIGLAND;
12. produce an explainable, temporally coherent economic graph;
13. pass a truthful FIRST_MAP readiness gate;
14. notify the subscriber when the first fruit is ready;
15. continue observing according to adaptive economic value/currentness;
16. make subsequent Xeeds cheaper and/or more intelligent because AXIGLAND already learned;
17. remain inside the external-observability boundary and explicitly mark private unknowables;
18. materialize answers as an explorable economic representation rather than a generated questionnaire report;
19. surface non-obvious but evidence-defensible representation signals and PATHXs;
20. explain every material surprising result through persisted evidence lineage;
21. detect representation anomalies without assuming they are either customer errors or AXIGNAL errors;
22. accept subscriber challenges without granting canonical edit authority;
23. independently reinvestigate challenged claims and return UPHELD, REVISED, RETIRED or UNRESOLVED;
