# AXIGNAL — Economic Discovery Engine Doctrine

**Status:** CANONICAL DOCTRINE PROPOSAL FOR MASTER INTEGRATION
**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Authority:** derives from MASTER §§14–16, 19, 35, 36, 53 and Constitution IV–X, XVI–XVII. Until merged into MASTER, any conflict is resolved in favor of MASTER.

## 1. Canonical chain

OBSERVE -> REPRESENT -> RETRIEVE -> FILTER -> COMPILE STATE -> BUILD CHOICE SPACE -> STRUCTURED EVALUATE -> INTERPRET -> INVESTIGATE -> ADMIT

AXIGNAL MUST explain both why it surfaced a discovery and at which boundary a true opportunity could have been lost. Every arrow is a versioned contract and a measurement boundary.

## 2. Authority boundaries
OBSERVE owns acquisition record/provenance/time/instrument, not truth admission. REPRESENT owns disposable semantic representation, not knowledge. RETRIEVE owns candidate recall/ranking, not fit/evidence. FILTER owns deterministic incompatibility rules and MUST preserve UNKNOWN. COMPILE STATE owns deterministic assembly/fingerprints, never invented missing facts. BUILD CHOICE SPACE owns AXIGNAL's typed hypothesis universe, not provider behavior. STRUCTURED EVALUATE owns typed probabilistic judgment, not policy/write. INTERPRET owns deterministic composition/policy, not evidence rewriting. INVESTIGATE owns targeted evidence acquisition, not canonical truth. ADMIT remains the independent canonical boundary; derived opportunity remains INXIGHT/POTENTIAL.

## 3. Representation/Retrieval Contract
Every semantic representation MUST retain canonical source/object reference, encoder/provider/model/version, dimension/normalization, content fingerprint, relevant locale, currentness dependency and index/config version. The index is disposable and reconstructable.

No k, bit-width, nprobe, similarity threshold or provider is doctrine. Retrieval MUST record returned candidates, rank, raw similarity/distance, configuration and later disposition. Benchmarks MUST measure recall against independently labelled/exact reference; precision-only measurement can hide lost opportunities.

## 4. Deterministic Filter Contract
A hard filter may reject only when a versioned deterministic rule has sufficient known inputs to demonstrate incompatibility within question scope. Missing/UNKNOWN/STALE MUST NOT become incompatible. Every rejection preserves rule_id, inputs and reason. Heuristic/probabilistic rejection MUST NOT masquerade as a hard filter.

## 5. State/Answerability Contract
Before structured judgment there MUST be DecisionContract, StateContract, InformationRequirements, deterministic assembly/normalization, state/question fingerprints and AnswerabilityGate. IDs/references without semantic content do not satisfy evidence requirements. State must be explicit, relevant and sufficiently complete to distinguish offered hypotheses. Missing material context leads to research or abstention, never a cheaper forced judgment.

## 6. Choice Space Contract
A Choice Space is a versioned AXIGNAL semantic asset, not incidental prompting. It MUST declare family/id/version, semantic target, stable option IDs, discriminating definitions, inclusion/exclusion boundaries, exclusivity, coverage policy, OTHER/NONE/UNKNOWN/UNRESOLVED where reality may fall outside listed alternatives, temporal/geographic/economic scope, invariants, allowed composition and semantic fingerprint.

The evaluator discriminates only among supplied alternatives. A winning choice does not prove coverage of reality. The full distribution MUST be retained for replay, comparison and reinterpretation.

## 7. Structured Evaluation Contract
JEV is currently a replaceable experimental STRUCTURED EVALUATE provider. Noul, Choice and Score are selected by question semantics. No universal SUPPORTED gate and no probability/confidence threshold is canonical. P>=0.50 does not mean opportunity, customer, relationship or probability of sale.

Provider result MUST preserve selected typed answer, full distribution where available, provider-defined confidence, model/version, state/question/choice fingerprints, latency/cost when observable and replay reference. None is canonical truth.

## 8. Deterministic Interpretation Contract
Python may derive top probability, P1-P2 margin, entropy/concentration, coverage/abstention, evidence/currentness/provenance dimensions and other explicitly versioned features. These are interpretation inputs, not truth. InterpretationPolicy is independently versioned and may output INVESTIGATE, POTENTIAL, UNKNOWN, abstention or another contract-defined state. No universal opaque opportunity score.

## 9. Attribution Ledger
Every run MUST reconstruct:
observation_set -> representation_set -> retrieved_set -> filtered_set -> judged_set -> interpreted_set -> investigated_set -> admitted_or_derived_set

Per boundary, where ground truth permits, measure coverage, positive retention, false rejection, precision, recall, cost, latency and cardinality delta. Candidate lineage records first-loss boundary and reason.

Minimum failure taxonomy: OBSERVATION_MISS, REPRESENTATION_LOSS, RETRIEVAL_MISS, FILTER_FALSE_REJECT, STATE_INSUFFICIENT, CHOICE_SPACE_MISS, EVALUATOR_MISCLASSIFICATION, INTERPRETATION_ERROR, INVESTIGATION_MISS, ADMISSION_REJECT, GROUND_TRUTH_AMBIGUOUS.

## 10. Baseline discipline
Architecture is implemented from authoritative documentation/contracts before optimization. Then freeze BASELINE V0. Only afterwards change variables. Development/calibration and sealed evaluation MUST be separated; sealed labels cannot tune prompts, Choice Spaces, thresholds or policy before evaluation.

Every run records dataset/version, configuration, seeds, encoder, index/provider/config, retrieval parameters, DecisionContracts, Choice Spaces, evaluator model/version, InterpretationPolicy and code SHA. A gain is attributed to a component only when relevant competing variables are controlled.
