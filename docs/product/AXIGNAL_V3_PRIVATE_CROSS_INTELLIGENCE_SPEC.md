# 0. Product definition

**AXIGNAL V3 --- Private Cross-Intelligence** adds an authorized private
analytical plane to AXIGNAL.

V1 reconstructs the externally observable economic world.

V2 systematically interrogates that external world.

V3 crosses that evidence-backed external reality with selected private
organizational data explicitly authorized by the customer.

Its central question is:

> **What becomes visible when AXIGNAL crosses how the organization
> exists from the outside with what is actually happening inside?**

Examples of private capabilities include:

-   CRM;
-   ERP;
-   internal knowledge;
-   documents;
-   analytics;
-   inventory;
-   authorized financial dimensions;
-   customer/supplier portfolios;
-   operational systems.

The intended reaction is:

> **"AXIGNAL has connected what is happening outside my company with
> things happening inside it that I had never connected."**

# 4. Three epistemic planes

## A. Public independent observation

Authority: AXIGLAND.

Examples:

-   public capabilities;
-   markets;
-   relationships;
-   corporate structure;
-   representation;
-   demand/supply signals;
-   temporal events;
-   public evidence.

## B. Customer-private observation

Authority: authorized private source.

Examples:

-   opportunities;
-   internal customers;
-   orders;
-   inventory;
-   internal product taxonomy;
-   internal documents;
-   private strategy;
-   authorized financial/operational dimensions.

## C. Private derived intelligence

Authority: tenant-scoped analytical projection.

Examples:

-   externally visible demand weakly represented in pipeline;
-   internally material capability weakly represented externally;
-   internal supplier concentration overlapping public graph
    dependencies;
-   public competitive structure differing from CRM loss data;
-   internal strategic priority with weak external corroboration.

The report must visually and semantically distinguish all three.

Linking private organizations to AXIGLAND is powerful and dangerous.

The link must preserve:

-   private source identity;
-   candidate public organization;
-   resolution evidence;
-   confidence/state;
-   contradictions;
-   customer assertions separately;

> "This CRM account corresponds to ACME Ltd."

# 26. Example cross-intelligence finding

### External demand is increasing in a cluster weakly represented in current pipeline

**External observation**\
AXIGNAL sees current demand signals around capability X.

**Private observation**\
Authorized CRM state shows limited opportunity representation in the
normalized cluster.

**Cross-intelligence interpretation**\
The discrepancy merits investigation.

**Alternative explanations** - taxonomy mismatch; - geographic
mismatch; - commercial prioritization; - incomplete CRM
classification; - stale private data; - external signal lacks commercial
relevance; - genuinely underexplored demand.

**What AXIGNAL does not know**\
Explicit unresolved dimensions.

**What could resolve it**\
Specific evidence/capability.

V3 must not jump to:
