## Interpretation

Evidence judgment, entity alignment, and relationship classification look semantically plausible in principle, but the present work establishes no measured opportunity or priority. The live P0-JEV-03 evidence is specifically unusable for claim-support performance because its state omitted claim and source text. Exact matching, temporal arithmetic, source permission, research priority, canonical admission and action execution remain deterministic or policy-owned. A good semantic fit never implies permission to mutate AXIGLAND.

Phase A official cookbooks cover cited-evidence assessment, entity alignment, and feature discovery (O12–O14); they offer patterns to test. E1/E2/E3 community repositories illustrate routing, dedupe, relevance and review mechanisms, but their outcomes are not AXIGNAL evidence. No candidate has been selected as roadmap winner.
