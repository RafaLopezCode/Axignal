# P0-DRI-00 — Digital Representation Intelligence

**Status:** Doctrine slice; proposed and pre-implementation.\
**Authority:** The single MASTER PRODUCT MODEL, §§22, 46 and 54.\
**Product spec:** [`AXIGNAL_DIGITAL_REPRESENTATION_INTELLIGENCE_PRODUCT_SPEC.md`](../../docs/product/AXIGNAL_DIGITAL_REPRESENTATION_INTELLIGENCE_PRODUCT_SPEC.md)\
**Spec Kit:** [`specs/013-p0-dri-00-digital-representation-intelligence/`](../../specs/013-p0-dri-00-digital-representation-intelligence/spec.md)

DRI observes search, generative, social/public-conversation and public
reputation/experience representations and connects them to a governed economic
world. Public Experience Intelligence is part of DRI, not a separate product.
Representation, reviews, ratings and classifications are not business truth.

This slice creates doctrine and measurement boundaries only. It authorizes no
runtime, source integrations, scraping, schema, provider, score, UI, live Jev,
deployment or experiment. The next recommended slice is P0-DRI-01 — Digital
Observation Contracts & Sensor Economics; it is not started here.
