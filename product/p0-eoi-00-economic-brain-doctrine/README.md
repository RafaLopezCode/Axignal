# P0-EOI-00 — Economic Brain Doctrine

**Status:** Doctrine slice complete; review/implementation gates remain external.\
**Authority:** The single MASTER PRODUCT MODEL, §§46 and 53.\
**Spec:** [`spec.md`](../../specs/012-p0-eoi-00-economic-brain-doctrine/spec.md)

AXIGNAL is an observing economic brain, not an economic index. Economic
Opportunity Intelligence and Compounding Economic Intelligence are inseparable
product pillars. Economic cartography is cognitive substrate; explainable,
temporal, reusable economic intelligence is the product.

This folder organizes the slice and does not establish a second doctrine
authority. See ADR-0012 for opportunity semantics and ADR-0013 for governed
compounding. This doctrine creates no runtime classes, source integrations,
schemas, provider authorization or production behavior. The next recommended
slice is P0-EOI-01 — Economic Reasoning Contracts; it is not started here.
