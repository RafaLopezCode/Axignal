# Decision Contract V-next

`DecisionContract` is the AXIGNAL-owned, provider-neutral declaration binding a semantic target to a question, primitive, answer space, information requirements, state contract, uncertainty semantics, composition policy, authority boundary and evaluation status. Contract identity is versioned independently from model, SDK, state compiler and policy.

Contract validity checks non-empty identity, matching family state version, unique answer-space members, compatible primitive and explicit authority/uncertainty/composition declarations. It does not certify truth, correctness, calibration or production readiness.

Choice requires meaningful options, declared mutual exclusivity and an explicit coverage strategy (`EXHAUSTIVE_WITH_OTHER` or `EXHAUSTIVE_WITH_UNRESOLVED`). Score requires one dimension with contiguous ordinal levels and definitions. Noul requires an explicit proposition and required argument paths. Primitive outputs and uncertainty must not be flattened into a universal confidence value.
