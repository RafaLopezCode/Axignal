# Naturalness and vNext.2 fit

## Definitions

- `NATURAL_CLAIM`: pre-existing human-authored claim, created independently of benchmark construction and not synthesized, paraphrased, negated, mutated, completed or simplified by the constructor.
- `NATURAL_EVIDENCE`: pre-existing human/document-authored evidence, not a template rendered from the same structured field used to define the expected answer.
- `PROCEDURAL_CLAIM`: deterministic text generated from structured fields; version and renderer hash must be retained.
- `PROCEDURAL_NEGATIVE`: a plausible, controlled same-domain transformation with explicit construction provenance; never merge with natural data.

## Route assessment

SEC filings and regulator orders provide real business documents and naturally authored content. But a proposed Route B must select person-free company claims, match them to the exact independent final finding, respect each document's rights, and then obtain independent human gold. The sources establish availability, not a proven eligible sample count or class balance. We therefore mark natural route plausible but not accepted.

GLEIF's Level 2 records are structured business relationship assertions, not natural claims or prose evidence. A deterministic renderer could assess supplied-evidence relation classification, provided the output is isolated as a procedural stratum. Template fingerprints and same-record label derivation must be disclosed and tested. Do not claim arbitrary-company reasoning from these cases.
