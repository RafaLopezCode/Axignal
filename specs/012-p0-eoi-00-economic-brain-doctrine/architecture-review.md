# Architecture Review: P0-EOI-00 Economic Brain Doctrine

**Outcome:** APPROVED AS DOCUMENTATION/DOCTRINE ONLY\
**Date:** 2026-09-26\
**Authority:** MASTER §§5, 14, 23, 46, 53; Constitution; ADR-0001–0011.

## Finding

AXIGNAL's existing product and architecture documents correctly preserve one
canonical AXIGLAND, evidence admission, independent epistemic authority,
provider abstraction and graph projections. Some user-facing and target
architecture prose still framed the graph as the product or canonized a
specific cognitive provider/model. Those statements conflict with the new
CTO-authorized MASTER doctrine and must be corrected or explicitly superseded.

## Decision

Adopt two inseparable product pillars: Economic Opportunity Intelligence and
Compounding Economic Intelligence. Make the map a cognitive projection, not
the final product. Keep demand/sensor/reach/opportunity concepts semantic only
in this slice. Treat opportunity as derived, temporal, explainable and
`POTENTIAL` by default; procurement or capability fit does not establish
commercial relationship. Require typed reasoning, deterministic-first routing,
answerability before judgment, AXENT research for missing context, and a
persisted ExplanationTrace. Reuse preserves provenance, epistemic state,
dependencies, currentness and history.

No evaluator/provider becomes the brain or canonical authority. Jev remains an
experimental replaceable candidate subject to independent authority and
rights; no live use is authorized. Do not create runtime classes, schemas,
questions, provider payloads, crawlers, endpoints or UI.

## Existing invariants preserved

One AXIGLAND; UNKNOWN is not FALSE; inference is not observation; POTENTIAL is
not OBSERVED; CLAIM is not WRITE; subscriber attention is not conclusion;
subscription buys observation, not influence; no CRM/workflow drift.

## Risks and boundaries

Product doctrine could be mistaken for current implementation or measured
capability. Documentation must explicitly distinguish future direction from
repository evidence. Reused erroneous knowledge can compound; provenance,
currentness, temporal dependencies and uncertainty remain inspectable. No
source rights, global coverage, commercial outcomes or provider performance
are claimed.
