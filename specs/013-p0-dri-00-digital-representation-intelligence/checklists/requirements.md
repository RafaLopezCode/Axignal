# Requirements Checklist: P0-DRI-00 Digital Representation Intelligence

## Four-family product doctrine

- [x] One MASTER defines search, generative, social/public conversation and
  public reputation/experience representation.
- [x] Public Experience Intelligence is part of DRI, not a separate product.
- [x] DRI is not SEO/GEO execution, social/review management or reputation
  repair.
- [x] Representation is condition-bound observation, not economic reality.

## Review and experience epistemics

- [x] ReviewObservation preserves source, ID, subject scope, native rating and
  scale, dates, observation time, source-reported verification, separate
  response, currentness and permitted lineage.
- [x] Review and reviewer claim are not business truth or FAXT.
- [x] Source rating and verification are not AXIGNAL judgment/authenticity.
- [x] Sentiment/theme are derived; anomaly is not fraud or review bombing.
- [x] Product/location/organization scope does not collapse.
- [x] ExperienceSignal is recurring, time-bounded and evidence-backed.
- [x] Removal changes currentness; removal does not establish falsity.
- [x] Duplicate/syndicated/cross-posted experiences are not double-counted.

## Measurement and score

- [x] Instrument, version, source/surface, conditions and time bind results.
- [x] Instrument drift does not become company change.
- [x] Sample, informative denominator, classification coverage and uncertainty
  survive aggregation.
- [x] Ratings across platforms are not naively compared or averaged.
- [x] Any future metric is deterministic, versioned, decomposable, inspectable,
  reproducible, source/currentness/uncertainty-aware and traceable.
- [x] Insufficient/incompatible evidence withholds a metric; no LLM-generated
  score or hidden weights.
- [x] Jev/evaluators classify bounded evidence only and remain replaceable and
  non-authoritative.

## Rights, privacy and reuse

- [x] Public visibility does not grant automated-collection rights.
- [x] Source identity, authority, access, terms, content/database rights,
  privacy, retention, deletion and provider transmission are future gates.
- [x] Authenticated scraping, cookie export and anti-bot bypass are not
  authorized by default.
- [x] Reviewer profiles are out of scope; raw text retention is not automatic.
- [x] Private first-party analytics remain tenant-private.
- [x] Authorized public observations may be reused across relevant Xignals
  without duplication, subject to rights and entity resolution.

## EOI, product, delivery and scope

- [x] Experience can trigger research; reviews alone do not create DemandSignal,
  FAXT or opportunity.
- [x] EOI may direct DRI observation and experience evidence may inform EOI
  reevaluation without collapsing epistemic boundaries.
- [x] Subscriber/AXENT/agency language is descriptive and traceable; no review
  response workflow or CRM drift.
- [x] P0-DRI-01 scope is documented but not started.
- [x] No runtime, provider, sensor integration, crawler, schema, migration,
  dependency, UI, experiment or deployment is included.
