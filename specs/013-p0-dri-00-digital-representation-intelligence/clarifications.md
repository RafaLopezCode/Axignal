# Clarifications: P0-DRI-00 Digital Representation Intelligence

These answers are frozen for this documentation slice and subordinate to
MASTER §54.

**Q: Is AXIGNAL an SEO or GEO tool?**  \
**A:** No. AXIGNAL independently observes representation; it does not execute
SEO/GEO campaigns or promise ranking outcomes.

**Q: Is a digital representation the underlying business reality?**  \
**A:** No. It records what an identified surface displayed under stated
conditions.

**Q: Are mention, citation and endorsement interchangeable?**  \
**A:** No. They are separate observations/interpretations and require distinct
evidence.

**Q: Is a review business truth or FAXT?**  \
**A:** No. It is evidence that a public experience claim was observed; the
underlying event requires independent evidence admission.

**Q: Does a platform's “verified” review mark prove the review's claim?**  \
**A:** No. Preserve the source-reported verification state without converting it
to AXIGNAL authenticity or truth.

**Q: Does a platform rating equal AXIGNAL's judgment?**  \
**A:** No. Preserve it as a source-native value with its scale, period and
source.

**Q: Does Jev produce a reputation score?**  \
**A:** No. A replaceable structured evaluator may classify bounded evidence.
Any future aggregate is deterministic, versioned, decomposable and traceable.

**Q: Can ratings from different platforms simply be averaged?**  \
**A:** No. Source populations, scales, coverage, geography and methods may be
incompatible.

**Q: Can a deleted review remain presented as currently active?**  \
**A:** No. Removal changes currentness. History may be retained only where
source rights permit; removal does not prove the claim false.

**Q: Does a spike in negative reviews prove review bombing or fraud?**  \
**A:** No. It may be a review-pattern anomaly and a research trigger.

**Q: Can recurring reviews reveal economic signals?**  \
**A:** Yes, as derived ExperienceSignals or AXENT research objectives; reviews
alone do not create canonical DemandSignals, FAXTs or opportunities.

**Q: Are all public review sources authorized for automated collection?**  \
**A:** No. Identity, authority, rights, access method, terms, privacy,
retention, deletion and provider transmission require source-specific review.

**Q: Does an owner-authenticated analytics connection make data public?**  \
**A:** No. Classification depends on acquisition channel and data. Private
first-party state remains tenant-private.

**Q: Does a percentage of reviews describe all customers?**  \
**A:** No. It describes eligible observed reviews in a defined window, with
sample, sources, classification coverage and uncertainty.

**Q: Can a company response prove the problem was resolved?**  \
**A:** No. A company response is a separate observation; resolution needs
independent supporting evidence.

**Q: Does a citation equal a mention?**  \\
**A:** No. A source may be cited without the organization being named.

**Q: Does an AI recommendation establish a business fact?**  \\
**A:** No. It establishes only what that identified surface displayed.

**Q: Does absence in one generative run mean zero visibility?**  \\
**A:** No. Runs may be non-informative or stochastic; preserve denominator,
replicates and uncertainty.

**Q: Can private Search Console or Analytics data enter public AXIGLAND because
the subscriber connected it?**  \\
**A:** No. Public independent evidence may separately establish a relevant
fact; the private connection alone does not.

**Q: Can one public observation enrich multiple organizations or Xignals?**  \\
**A:** Yes, if rights-cleared, independently resolved, deduplicated and
epistemically admissible.

**Q: Can authenticated scraping or browser evasion be used by default?**  \\
**A:** No. Future use requires explicit authority, rights and security review.

**Q: Can AXIGNAL expose one universal SEO/GEO/social score?**  \\
**A:** No. A future source-aware metric must be deterministic, versioned,
decomposable and traceable; opaque universal scoring is prohibited.

**Q: Can DRI generate an Economic Opportunity by itself?**  \\
**A:** No. It can produce signals, gaps and research objectives for EOI to
investigate.
