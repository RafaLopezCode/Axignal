## Global copy rules

- Meaning before ontology.
- No claim may be stronger in Spanish than in English.
- `POTENTIAL` never becomes an observed customer, lead, purchase or relationship.
- `UNKNOWN` never becomes a negative fact.
- AXENT never becomes canonical truth authority.
- Xeed is the planted persistent-observation seed; one Xeed may produce many Xignals. Xignals are not billable units or canonical writes.
- The artwork and copy must complement each other rather than narrate the same information.
- CTA language must be low-friction and must not imply a working checkout if billing is not live.
