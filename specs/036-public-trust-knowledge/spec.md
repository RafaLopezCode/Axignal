## User scenarios and testing
### US1 (P1): Learn through the Observador's editorial notebook
A visitor browses a substantive Knowledge index, filters/searches explanations, reads a permalink article, follows its doctrine source and reaches an adjacent article or the illustrative Panorama.
Acceptance: accessible search/category controls; honest empty results with reset; six complete ES/EN articles explain concepts without fabricated economic facts, metrics, authors or news. Reading time derives from actual words. Unknown article returns 404.
### US2 (P1): Understand trust and prepare a question
A visitor browses the policy hub and privacy/terms/cookies documents; sees draft status and missing operator facts; selects a contact subject and prepares a message.
Acceptance: visible publication-pending information; linked sources; no invented company/retention basis/contact mailbox. Form validates, previews exact text and downloads a local draft; never claims transmission. Draft remains only in this mounted page, not localStorage or server.
### US3 (P1): Understand and prepare sign-in
A new/returning visitor uses Login/Signup, sees Google and Continue with ChatGPT with equal prominence, reads narrow identity permissions and sees truthful availability.
Acceptance: real presentation-only start endpoint returns 503 until an owning AuthenticationPort is connected; strict payload and same-origin guard; no cookies, sessions, secrets, OAuth tokens, paid access or Admin privilege. Recovery offers a clearly separate demo.
### US4 (P1): Understand personal-data rights
A visitor sees a GDPR rights explainer, selects a right and prepares/downloads a request addressed to the future controller. Distinguishes personal data, observation focus and shared economic memory.
Acceptance: no automatic deletion or invented legal immunity of public evidence; conditional rights applicability; controller must assess actual request; no false lodged-request receipt.
