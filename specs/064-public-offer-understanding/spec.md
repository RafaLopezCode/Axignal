# Feature Specification: Public offer understanding

**Feature Branch**: `feat/bidirectional-constructive-intelligence-sol61`
**Created**: 2026-10-09
**Status**: Implementation authorized by CTO task; review pending
**Input**: AXIGNAL bidirectional intelligence execution goal, activated explicitly for Sol.

## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - Understand public communication (Priority: P1)
A subscriber or agency sees what an external instrument can understand about an offer, intended audience and promised outcome, with strengths and conditional constructive gaps at equal depth.
**Independent Test**: A controlled public site produces exact navigable citations, a strength, an omission and a conditional proposal; classification readiness does not suppress this measurement.
**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. Clear offer/audience/outcome: strengths, no mandatory negative finding.
2. Minimal shoe repair communication: limited comprehension, no invented capabilities or marketing claims.
3. A failed acquisition, uncertain judgment or failed instrument control yields investigation/unknown, never a company defect.
4. Conflicting pages remain unresolved and both sources remain inspectable.

### User Story 2 - Reobserve and compare (Priority: P1)
The subscriber inspects prior conditioned results and understands whether an interpretation changed under comparable conditions.
**Independent Test**: Persist two source versions, reopen storage, inspect a comparable difference; changed model/method/surface/language breaks comparison.
**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. A revised statement changes only affected source state and is measured anew.
2. Unchanged exact state may reuse a valid judgment, explicitly recorded as reuse rather than another independent replica.
3. Tenant/client scope, retention and revoked attention prevent another client's results or expired quotations being exposed.

### Edge Cases
Robots refusal, source unavailability, skipped required page, extraction truncation, personal/contact data, missing provider input rights, provider failure, invalid response, uncertain distribution, deliberate limited disclosure, instrument drift, expired citations and replaced Focus.

## Requirements
- FR-001: Separate public comprehension from economic activity/demand routing and canonical truth.
- FR-002: Preserve source URLs, exact short quotations, fingerprints, times, rights, coverage, instrument/question/interpretation versions, locale and actual judgment distribution in an authorized trace.
- FR-003: Distinguish representation limitation, acquisition insufficiency and instrument error; uncertainty alone cannot blame the organization.
- FR-004: Proposals are dimension-specific and conditional, cite inspected evidence and alternatives, and never guarantee discovery/ranking/sales.
- FR-005: Source/provider rights and budgets precede evaluation; no tenant/private attention is transmitted to a provider.
- FR-006: Retain tenant-private, bounded history; compare compatible executions only and expose reuse/currentness truthfully.
- FR-007: Provide directly navigable evidence and history in the existing subscriber surface, usable at desktop/narrow widths in supported languages.
- FR-008: No price, canonical write, production flag or production deployment change.

### Key Entities
Conditioned offer report; exact citation; dimension judgment; acquisition coverage; temporal comparison; instrument control.

## Success Criteria
- SC-001: All six requested adversarial scenarios have repeatable acceptance evidence.
- SC-002: Every visible strength/critique resolves to inspected evidence; an unavailable/error measurement produces no company critique.
- SC-003: A subscriber can inspect the report and its citations/history at desktop and narrow widths.
- SC-004: Restarted storage preserves isolation and compatible history without retaining citations beyond their permitted lifetime.

## Assumptions
First vertical: own public website, three narrow offering dimensions. No search/reputation/social or consumer-product simulation claims. Synthetic source/provider tests are labeled; real-client quality is not inferred from them. Reuse incumbent authorization, rights, asynchronous acquisition and economic reasoning. Human visual acceptance remains CTO review.
