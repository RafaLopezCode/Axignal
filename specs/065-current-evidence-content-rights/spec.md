# Spec 065 — Current evidence content rights

Authority: MASTER §54.4/§55; Constitution; ADR-0015, ADR-0050, ADR-0088,
ADR-0091; issue #176. Base: origin/main 987ca9168fcad9a8aa12d43f6ec1386d0ca2abab.

## Outcome

Revocation or expiry of registered source-content rights takes effect at the
next authorized read and reuse, including previously materialized economic
outputs, subscriber, AXENT, MCP and historical cuts, without service restart.
Original admission, non-verbatim economic knowledge and lawful provenance remain
historical authorities. No frontend, evaluator, canonical-fact mutation or deployment.

## Requirements

- Current content permission is distinct from recorded admission and currentness.
- An absent/unknown/revoked grant never upgrades an originally registered source
  to an unregistered private baseline. Retention cannot extend the original grant.
- Check once at a shared evidence delivery/reuse boundary, after membership and
  organization authorization. Materialized snapshots must pass the same boundary.
- Preserve independent authorized sources, tenants, canonical facts and historical
  identifiers/timestamps. UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.
- No reusable verbatim content may escape through ledgers, narratives, cached
  projections, continuity, source references, AXENT or MCP after withdrawal.

## Acceptance

Deterministic real composition: register → observe/admit → persist → read web,
AXENT and MCP → revoke → reread same service. Assert text withdrawn, economic
knowledge/provenance retained and other sources/tenants unaffected. Cover expiry,
reobservation, old snapshots, purge and cross-tenant denial. No LLM/network in gates.
