## Direction

An observer's notebook: one clear finding in an open, inspectable sheet.
Fraunces carries human meaning; existing body type carries controls. AXIGNAL
blue #354f98 anchors attention, incumbent paper #fafbfc gives reading space,
existing sage/violet/brass retain labelled states. Global 1600px frame stays.
The focal interaction is the visitor changing date or attention lane, then
opening the source-backed explanation. This makes observation inspectable;
it does not decorate a generic dashboard or simulate real-time research.

helper over existing insights. Use existing NatureMark, InsightBody and the
synthetic evidence context. Native controls choose date/lane/finding and open
evidence inline; no modal or animation library is required. CSS gives the
sheet one bounded entrance and user-triggered transitions, static by default
under reduced motion. No autoplay, canvas, timers or new network dependency.

Replace the long scrolly sequence with directly selectable step explanations
and one working example. Preserve the explanatory copy and all downstream
proof/audience/pricing sections, tighten only their landing-specific spacing.
Decorative narrator assets remain governed and are not product truth.
