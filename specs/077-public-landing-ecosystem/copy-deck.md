## Ten-family catalog

| Family | Human question | Evidence context | Material limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| presence | ¿Cómo aparece y se entiende tu empresa? | Own public web; search and generative surfaces when actually measured | Conditions, instrument and time matter; no Internet-wide visibility guarantee. |
| reputation | ¿Qué se dice y quién lo dice? | Authorized public mentions and experience sources | A review is an observed expression, not business truth or an endorsement. |
| value | ¿Qué ofrece y qué sabe hacer? | Public offer, declared capabilities, credentials | A published claim is not independent proof of performance. |
| markets | ¿Dónde opera y cómo llega? | Capability-specific delivery and reach statements | Headquarters do not define the whole market; unknown reach remains unknown. |
| relationships | ¿Qué vínculos tienen evidencia? | Explicit public customers, partners and suppliers | A project, award or path does not establish a direct customer or subcontract. |
| demand | ¿Qué actividad podría ser relevante? | Public needs, projects and procurement with requirements | Derived relevance is potential, not a lead, client or guaranteed fit. |
| activity | ¿Qué ocurre y qué cambia? | Published work, announcements and observable changes | Event time, observation time and currentness stay distinct. |
| economics | ¿Qué cifras se pueden conocer? | Available public economic figures with period and source | Missing revenue/scale is unknown; no private financial visibility. |
| organization | ¿Quién es esta organización? | Economic identity and public identity statements | Observation is not ownership or an editable company profile. |
| context | ¿Qué entorno ayuda a interpretarla? | Relevant external public documents, rules and sector context | Context is scoped by evidence and capability; no universal forecast. |

## Industry journeys

### Industry and manufacturing

Argument: conecta capacidades, actividad y demanda pública antes de investigar una oportunidad. Questions: what does it manufacture or supply; which public requirements might fit those capabilities; what delivery/reach evidence is missing? Families: value, activity, demand, markets, relationships. Limits: no assumed plant capacity, private supply-chain visibility or commercial fit.

### Construction and engineering

Argument: comprende proyectos y requisitos desde lo que la organización puede justificar. Questions: which public projects deserve reading; what credentials/requirements matter; where can it deliver? Families: demand, value, markets, activity, context. Limits: an award is not a subcontract; a distant tender does not prove expansion.

### Energy

Argument: lee la actividad pública de la transición energética con contexto y procedencia. Questions: what capabilities and announced projects are visible; what public need could align; which contextual changes require verification? Families: activity, value, demand, markets, context. Limits: no forecast of project execution, funding or regulatory eligibility without evidence.

### Logistics

Argument: diferencia ubicación, servicio y alcance antes de interpretar la demanda. Questions: which delivery modes are declared; what service areas are supported; what requirements or uncertainty affect a possible match? Families: markets, value, relationships, demand, context. Limits: no live fleet tracking, invented routes, private operating data or distance-based radius assumptions.

### Professional services and consultancies

Argument: prepara una lectura fundamentada de las organizaciones a las que asesoras. Questions: how is an offer described; what changes since the prior reading; what can be explained with source/date and what remains open? Families: organization, value, activity, economics, context. Limits: missing financial data remains unknown; no automated professional advice or client-truth editing.

### Agencies and digital communication

Argument: distingue lo que una empresa hace de lo que su presencia pública deja entender. Questions: what offer/audience/outcome are understood from inspected pages; where are there strengths or conditional gaps; are previous measurements comparable? Families: presence, reputation, value, markets, activity. Limits: search/generative coverage only when actually measured; no SEO execution, ranking guarantee, reputation management or private analytics promise.

## Three audience entrances

Company: understand your organization and its environment. Agency: explain public representation and changes across client organizations. Consultancy: make an evidence-backed reading before advising. The same product serves all three; a role changes the question, not the evidence.

## Method and proof

Choose an organization → AXIGNAL observes available public sources and retains context → inspect changes, the source and what remains uncertain. AXENT explains from accumulated context; it does not turn a prompt into canonical truth. Public illustration is not evidence. The canonical guided example remains the only fictional dataset, clearly labeled at its actual destination.
