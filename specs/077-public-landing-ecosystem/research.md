## Page argument / proof

1. Understand change around organizations → explain observation, memory and inspectable evidence in the first viewport; link the real canonical example.
2. One business has several connected dimensions → ten-family catalog using the existing IDs, with substantive questions, source types and limits.
3. The same evidence serves different decisions → distinct industry questions mapped to the relevant families, explicitly editorial rather than invented success stories.
4. Knowledge becomes useful over time → explain retained history/currentness and directly accessible evidence, without fabricating a timeline of a real company.
5. Different buyers can use the same product → company, agency and consultancy paths; no separate truth, private-data leakage or invented product plan.
6. Access and cost are understandable → governed price book and current Design Partner programme, real example/signup/contact exits.

## Ordered structural candidates

1. Economic reading: a full-width readable product argument, families as an index and a first decision made from context.
2. Family explorer: begin with the ten questions; open the selected family into its evidence and limits.
3. Industry lens: the visitor chooses an industry, then sees its questions and connected data families.
4. Temporal memory: a source, the accumulated context and the current reading compose one continuous mechanism.
5. Evidence atelier: source → family → meaningful question, with the evidence/context mechanism at true reading size.
6. Question directory: practical questions lead to the relevant families and use cases without architectural vocabulary.
7. Capability atlas: a labeled conceptual map of the ten families around an organization, then sector entrances.
