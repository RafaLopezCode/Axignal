# Art direction and motion sequence
The previous homepage and LandingFocus are functional references only.

## Three distinct directions
A — Editorial Intelligence: asymmetrical editorial spread, large Fraunces proposition, indexed reading and a single annotated finding. Strong clarity and accessible delivery; weaker sense of a living economic world.
B — Living Economic World: a navigable field of organizations, source nodes and meaningful connections. Strong exploration; risks early cognitive load, weak mobile density and graph-first misunderstanding.
C — The Observer's Journey: an open investigation desk. Scattered public clues become a contextual possibility, then inspectable proof and temporal understanding. Strong brand specificity and commercial comprehension; must keep navigation direct.

Selected C, with A's editorial legibility and a bounded B-style connection diagram. Evaluation is a design judgment, not measured conversion evidence. It best balances first-five-second meaning, agency/consultancy relevance, original composition, product demonstration, touch/keyboard access, responsive delivery and maintainability.

## Shape
Hero: editorial statement beside a live, typographic evidence constellation; the original Observer watches from the margin. No browser frame or subscriber panel.
Discovery: choose a professional question, read the result on a broad open sheet, compare temporal observations, inspect reasoning and source excerpts. No dashboard replica.
Professional lens: three workflow projections over the same method. Then an illustrated horizontal observation loop, a quiet evidence/trust argument, transparent portfolio pricing, useful FAQ and one closing invitation.
Palette: canonical blue #354F98, charcoal #3C3C3C, pale paper #F8FAFC, white, muted sage #E5EEE8 and violet #EBE8F4. Fraunces for editorial voice, Manrope for action/reading, IBM Plex Mono for dates only.

## GSAP narrative
1. Observe: after the visitor begins exploring and the essential content has painted, the intact Observer illustration shifts subtly toward the existing source constellation. No anatomy deformation or new gesture invention.
2. Connect: entering the bounded ecosystem section emphasizes existing source → capacity → potential connections and brings their labels into focus. Static HTML shows the complete meaning from the start.
3. Remember: selecting an actual fixture date changes semantic state in React; a short opacity/transform transition explains the changed context. No invented progress or signals.
4. Explain: opening proof gently emphasizes the source list; native details and keyboard remain authoritative.
A single natural-scroll sequence, no pinning/scrubbing, no loops, no effect on CTA availability. Reduced motion never loads the animation module. Preference changes remove it and revert its context.
